## Hypotheses to pre-register
All ensembles have 8 pairs, size 400, `scene=gui` and `ghost=all` unless stated. α = 0.01.

- **H1, reference match.**
  - Derived from: a research-phase Python port matched the reference means, (96.6, 100.0, 70.9) vs (97.0, 100.2, 71.0).
  - Claim: for every diffuse region r (`DIFFUSE_REGION_IDS`) and channel, the reference's region mean lies inside the
    `chain=image`, P=256 ensemble's prediction interval.
  - Interval level: 1−α/m (Bonferroni, where m = the number of region×channel pairs). Use `ensemble.prediction_interval` on the
    per-render region means of *single* renders, a8 and b8 separately, so 16 samples.
- **H2, threads are irrelevant.**
  - Derived from: research found the Linux single-stream and Windows OpenMP builds statistically identical.
  - Claim: `chain=image` vs `chain=omp-restart:4` and vs `chain=omp-restart:16` at P=64 are indistinguishable on all metric keys (`compare_ensembles`).
- **H3, generator wrap is irrelevant.**
  - Derived from: the incremental Halton generator is periodic mod 2^23 with error ≤ 7.5e-8.
  - Claim: `halton=exact` vs `incremental`, `chain=image`, P=64, are indistinguishable on all keys.
- **H4, converged texture.**
  - Derived from: research found the reference to be a converged, deterministic pattern.
  - Claim: `all.pattern_corr` increases with P ∈ {16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512}. Test adjacent P pairs one-sided with `separated` and Holm.
  - The M1 review measured the seed-independent back-wall structure std at 50 passes as 5.76, against the reference's *total* high-pass
    std of 5.78. That suggests the reference is nearly converged, far beyond 50 passes.
  - Also estimate the reference's effective pass count:
    1. Fit the back-wall (region 4) high-pass variance of single renders as `v(P) = σs² + σn²/P` by least squares over the 6 P values.
    2. Solve `v(P_ref) = var_ref`, where `var_ref` is the reference's back-wall high-pass variance.
    3. Report P_ref with a bootstrap 95% interval over pairs (`jax.random.PRNGKey(0)`, 1000 resamples).
- **H5, loop order matters.**
  - Derived from: research found pass-major correlates 0.51–0.63 with the reference and pixel-major 0.06–0.20.
  - Claim: `chain=pixel-major` at P=64 has a significantly lower `all.pattern_corr` than `chain=image` (`separated`, "less").
