## Modify
- `analysis/painterly_analysis/report.py`
  - `summary_table(runs: Mapping[str, Sequence[Mapping[str, float]]], keys) -> {name: {key: {"mean", "sd"}}}`, with sd
    computed as ddof=1. It replaces the per-experiment `summary_table`/`_summary` copies.
  - `family_test(..., labels=(a, b))` gains a `comparison` field, as `directional_pair_test` already has. The 002-006
    label-only `_family_test` wrappers then become direct calls.
  - `check_seed_reuse(ensembles, earlier_jobs: Mapping[str, Path], reused: Mapping[str, tuple[str, str]])`. A seed may be
    shared with an earlier experiment only when the option sets are identical. It replaces the per-experiment checks
    (003 `_check_design`'s reuse part, 004, 006).
  - `mosaic(previews: Sequence[np.ndarray], columns: int)`. The Mapping argument's keys were ignored. Update every caller
    and test.
  - One `format_number(value, spec)` in `report.py` replaces the `_fmt` copies, where their behaviour is identical. Where
    it differs, keep the local copy and say why in a comment.
- `analysis/tests/test_report.py`: tests for each new or changed function, with exact equality.
- `experiments/00{2,3,4,6}-*/analyze.py`: use them.
